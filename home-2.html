Patient Advisory

Frequently Asked Questions

Get fast answers to common questions about appointments, insurance, and maternity stay.

Q1

When should I schedule my first prenatal visit?

We recommend booking your first ultrasound and consultation around weeks 6 to 8 after your last period.

Q2

Do you accept my health insurance provider?

We partner with over 35 major private insurance networks and Medicare with upfront financial transparency.

Q3

Can partners stay overnight in maternity suites?

Yes, all of our private luxury postpartum suites include dedicated overnight companion accommodations.

Q4

What happens during an emergency at night?

Our clinical triage unit operates 24/7 with on-site obstetricians ready for immediate emergency intervention.
