What to Bring to Your Visit

Identification

Please bring a valid, government-issued photo ID (Driver's License, Passport, or State ID) to verify your patient file.

Insurance Card

Ensure you have your most recent physical or digital health insurance card for billing coordination at the front desk.

Medication List

Bring a written list of any current prescriptions, over-the-counter medications, and prenatal vitamins you are actively taking.
